Build the messaging screen for Northbeam, a revenue-analytics SaaS. This is the team inbox where staff discuss accounts with each other.

Layout: a conversation list on the left (~1/3 width) and the open conversation on the right.

The conversation list shows ~8 threads: customer or teammate name, last message preview, timestamp, and unread state. The open thread shows a realistic back-and-forth about a customer account (e.g. a churn-risk discussion with numbers), plus a composer at the bottom (text input and send button).

Details that make it feel real: today/yesterday timestamps, an unread badge count, an online indicator, a "typing…" or pending state if you like, and a thread header with the account name and a link to its dashboard.

Mock data inline. No backend, no external services.

Polished, professional, ready to ship. Everything in English.
