Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Starter and Growth include a free trial with full access to every feature in the plan. Scale includes a guided setup with our team, so you can see the plan on your own revenue data.

How does annual billing work?

You can switch to annual billing at any time. You're billed once per year at 20% off the monthly rate; the per-month price shown is the monthly equivalent.

Can we add seats later?

Yes. Starter includes up to 5 seats and Growth up to 25. If you outgrow a plan, upgrade in settings — or contact sales for a custom arrangement on Scale.

Do you support invoicing and purchase orders?

Annual Scale contracts can be invoiced and support purchase orders. Contact sales and we'll set up billing that fits your procurement process.

Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes. Annual plans are billed once per year. For invoicing, SSO, or custom terms, contact sales.
