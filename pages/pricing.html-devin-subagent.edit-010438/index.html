Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card required. During the trial you get full access to Growth-tier features, so you can evaluate Northbeam on your real revenue data before choosing a plan.

Can I switch plans or billing periods later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and plan changes take effect immediately with prorated billing. Switching from annual to monthly billing applies at your next renewal date. Your remaining annual term is honored first.

What counts as a data source?

Any system you connect to Northbeam: Stripe, Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, your data warehouse, or a custom integration. Starter includes up to 3 sources; Growth and Scale include unlimited sources.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Early-stage startups under $2M ARR qualify for 50% off their first year, and registered nonprofits receive 30% off any plan. Reach out to sales@northbeam.io and we'll set it up for you.

Prices in USD. Applicable taxes may be added at checkout. Questions about enterprise pricing or custom contract terms? Contact our sales team.
