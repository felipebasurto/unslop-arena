Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. You won't be charged until it ends, and you can cancel at any time.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade from your billing settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, and we prorate the difference.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is a teammate who can sign in to your workspace. People who only view a shared dashboard don't use a seat.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Annual billing already saves 20% on every plan. For larger rollouts or special pricing, contact sales.
