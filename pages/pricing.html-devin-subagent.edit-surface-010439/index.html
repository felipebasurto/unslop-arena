Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes, every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to the Growth feature set. No credit card required. When the trial ends, pick the plan that fits or keep exploring on Starter.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

What counts as a seat and a data source?

A seat is any team member with login access to Northbeam. View-only guests don’t count. A data source is any connected system, such as your billing platform, CRM, or data warehouse.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Early-stage startups under $1M ARR and registered nonprofits can apply for 50% off any plan for the first year. Reach out to sales@northbeam.io and we’ll set it up.

All prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes. Need a custom plan, invoiced billing, or volume pricing? Talk to sales.
