Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

What counts as a seat?

A seat is a teammate who can sign in to Northbeam. Viewers and integrations don't count toward the limit.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and we prorate the difference. Downgrades start at your next billing period.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Early-stage startups and nonprofits can contact sales for custom pricing.

All prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes. Need a custom plan? Contact sales.
