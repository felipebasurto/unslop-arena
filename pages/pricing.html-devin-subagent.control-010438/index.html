Questions, answered

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial of Growth — full dashboards, forecasting, and integrations. No credit card required, and you can downgrade or cancel before the trial ends.

Can I change plans or cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated; downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of your current billing cycle. Annual plans can be refunded within the first 30 days.

How are revenue events counted?

Each row synced from your billing system, CRM, warehouse, or product analytics counts as one revenue event. Starter includes 10,000 events per month, Growth includes 100,000, and Scale is unlimited — you can monitor usage anytime in Settings.

Do you offer discounts or enterprise terms?

Early-stage startups under $2M ARR get 50% off Growth for their first year. For procurement, custom contracts, security reviews, or volume pricing, contact our sales team at sales@northbeam.com.

Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes. Need SSO, custom contracts, or procurement terms? Contact our sales team.
