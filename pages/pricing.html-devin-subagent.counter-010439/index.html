Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and full access to its features. We do not ask for a credit card, and if you do nothing the trial simply ends.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your workspace settings. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of the billing period. Charges are prorated automatically.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data remains exportable for 30 days after cancellation through CSV downloads and warehouse syncs. After that window it is permanently deleted under our data retention policy.

Do you offer discounts for early-stage startups?

Yes. Companies with fewer than 50 employees can get 50% off their first year on any plan. Email sales@northbeam.com and we will set it up.

Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes. Sales tax and VAT are calculated at checkout based on your billing address.
